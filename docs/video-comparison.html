Video goals vs our design delivery

How xjet-swarma-video answers the creator-owned video proposal in At last, something bigger and better than YouTube by xjet.

Comparison date: 27 November 2026 · Video duration: approximately 18:37 · Source: English auto-generated captions

Design delivery is not shipped software. This repository currently contains product and architecture documentation, not an application runtime. Every capability below is a design commitment, partial proposal, or gap—not a verified implementation.

Overall: we retain creator-owned hosting and distributed delivery, but deliberately replace portal-injected advertising with creator-controlled access paths and separate delivery funding. Turnkey appliance requirements are now documented; hardware validation and software delivery are still pending. Independent portals remain a design gap.

What closes the largest gaps?

  1. Turnkey creator appliance: requirements now documented in §6.10 and staged in Phases 0–1; next deliver the support matrix, installer/images, fallback integration, and measured first-publish/recovery results.
  2. Independent portals: creator enrollment, publish notifications, human curation, portable audience relationships, and a clear boundary between editorial choice and abuse enforcement.
  3. Validated economics and delivery: demonstrate payments, peer/edge incentives, bounded origin load, and realistic operating costs without assuming portal advertising funds the system.

The appliance row reflects the added design commitment; other suggested gap closures are comparison findings, not automatic scope changes. The high-level design remains the source of truth.
